Our Method

Tree to Bottle in 24 Hours

How we preserve the fresh, raw character of Alphonso mangoes without losing heat-sensitive nutrients.

Handpicked at Peak Ripeness

Our partners harvest each mango by hand. Only mangoes that have achieved perfect skin color and aroma are selected, ensuring the ultimate brix level (sweetness) for our juice.

  • Sourced directly from Ratnagiri, India
  • Naturally ripened without chemicals
  • Rigorous quality inspection on harvest

Cold Pressing at 4°C

Pasteurization destroys flavor and essential nutrients. We extract the juice using cold-press technology, applying gentle pressure at low temperatures to retain fiber and nutrients.

  • No heat generated during extraction
  • Retains active Vitamin C and antioxidants
  • Thick, velvety, full-bodied texture

High-Pressure Processing (HPP)

Instead of boiling the juice to kill bacteria, we subject the bottled juice to 6,000 atmospheres of cold water pressure. This eliminates pathogens while keeping the taste and nutrition 100% raw.

  • No preservatives needed
  • Extends shelf life up to 45 days
  • Retains pure fresh flavor and aroma

Guaranteed Cold Chain

From our HPP chamber to your refrigerator, the temperature is strictly maintained between 2°C and 4°C. This ensures the raw enzymes remain active and the taste stays orchard-fresh.

  • Ships in insulated, chilled containers
  • Real-time temperature tracking
  • Next-day express delivery

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers. Everything you need to know about our cold-pressed process.

High-Pressure Processing (HPP) is a cold pasteurization technique where the bottled juice is placed inside a hyperbaric chamber and subjected to extremely high water pressure (up to 6,000 bars). This inactivates yeast, mold, and bacteria without heat, preserving the raw nutrients, flavor, and color of the fresh fruit.

Absolutely not! RAW Anizip juices contain 100% fruit. There are zero added sugars, chemical preservatives, colors, artificial flavors, or even added water. It is as pure as eating a fresh Alphonso mango straight from the tree.

Because the juice is raw and contains active enzymes, it must be kept refrigerated at all times (between 2°C and 4°C). Under proper refrigeration, unopened bottles stay fresh for up to 45 days. Once opened, keep it refrigerated and consume within 48 hours.

We source our mangoes directly from cooperative farms in Ratnagiri, Devgad, and the Konkan region of Maharashtra, India. This geographic area is world-famous for producing the highest-quality Alphonso mangoes, known for their distinct golden saffron hue and rich, fiberless pulp.
